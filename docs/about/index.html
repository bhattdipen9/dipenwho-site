My path into finance

Born and raised in Nepal, I first trained as a civil engineer. Investing drew me toward valuation and financial research, and eventually to graduate study in the United States. I bring an understanding of physical assets to questions about capital, risk, and growth.

I began following and investing in stocks in 2020. What started as an interest in markets became a desire to understand what a business is worth, why expectations change, and where a differentiated view might come from. That curiosity led me to the CFA Program and a career in investment analysis.

Where I bring perspective

At Gen Alpha Private Limited, I worked on investment analysis, valuation, and financial and technical due diligence across infrastructure and renewable energy. My engineering background helps me think about the assets behind the model: how they are built, how they operate, and what can change their cash flows.

As a valuation consultant for Aadhya School, I developed a ten-year FCFE model and enrollment scenarios to support expansion and capital-raising analysis.

What I’m exploring now

I am completing an MS in Quantitative Finance at Washington University in St. Louis’s Olin Business School, with graduation expected in December 2026. My projects connect fundamental research with Python, data analysis, and an interest in applying AI to financial research workflows.

Beyond the work

I enjoy playing guitar, traveling, watching films, and reading about philosophy, psychology, and markets. I like meeting people and learning how their experiences shape the way they think.
